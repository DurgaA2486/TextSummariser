The web-based evaluation tool is specifically designed to assess the quality of automatically generated summaries, with a primary objective of determining whether these summaries accurately represent the important information in the original content while maintaining coherence, relevance, and accuracy. To achieve this, the tool evaluates the generated summaries on three key dimensions: content, structure, and tone. A good summary should preserve essential meaning without repeating information or introducing unsupported facts, ensuring that users receive a concise yet informative overview of the source material. The optimal length of a summary is a crucial factor in achieving these goals; the recommended maximum summary length is 150 words, which can be adapted based on different source document sizes to accommodate a broader range of content types.

Different testing scenarios test the behavior of the application in various conditions, such as high-quality summaries, incomplete or inaccurate summaries, overly long summaries with repeated information, and those containing errors or hallucinations. These tests validate the tool's ability to detect issues in generated summaries and provide feedback on how improvements can be made. The test sequences are designed to mimic real-world use cases, allowing users to assess the performance of the application under various scenarios.

The comparison phase is a critical component of the evaluation process. During this stage, the tool compares each generated summary against its source content, calculating metrics for accuracy, completeness, relevance, conciseness, coherence, and scores indicating potential issues such as the use of unsupported or potentially hallucinated information. The assessment results provide detailed information on the evaluation process of summaries, including word count differences between the original and the summary, final assessment status, detected issues, omitted important details, and clear recommendations for improvement.

The proposed application is designed to be adaptable, allowing users to adjust its parameters seamlessly based on different scenarios. This flexibility enables the tool to respond effectively under various conditions by adjusting its evaluation standards without compromising accuracy or reliability. The application distinguishes between different levels of evaluation based on strictness from a more lenient approach, providing users with flexibility and control over the assessment process.

Ultimately, this web-based evaluation tool has the potential to significantly improve the quality of automatically generated summaries, enabling users to receive more accurate and informative content in real-time, thereby improving the efficiency and effectiveness of information dissemination.
